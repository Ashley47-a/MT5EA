Advanced Forex Trading Robot for MT5

Project Overview

A modular MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor designed to analyze Forex markets, generate trading signals, manage risk, and support automated trading after testing.

Initial Features

- Market trend analysis
- Multi-timeframe confirmation
- Buy, Sell, and No Trade signals
- Stop-loss and take-profit management
- Position sizing and risk controls
- Trade logging
- Strategy Tester compatibility

Development Plan

1. Define the trading strategy.
2. Develop the MQL5 Expert Advisor.
3. Compile and resolve errors.
4. Backtest the strategy.
5. Test on a demo account.
6. Evaluate performance before considering live trading.

Safety

Live trading will remain disabled during initial development and testing.
